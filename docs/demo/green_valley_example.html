One small watershed, from setup to reach response.

This 15.8 km² subwatershed keeps the example readable while exercising the complete workflow: a prepared 50 m grid, daily recharge, paired unimpaired and pumped simulations, outlet accounting, and results for all 37 stream reaches. Use the views below to inspect the figures produced by the CLI.

What this page is—and is not

A result viewer, not a browser-based solver.

The model still runs from the versioned CLI configuration, where paths, units, assumptions, preflight checks, and provenance remain explicit. This page is a portable tour of its outputs. It is suitable for GitHub Pages and works without Python, a backend, or geospatial libraries.
